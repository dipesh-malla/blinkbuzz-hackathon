BlinkBuzz Hackathon: a hackathon series in Nepal

BlinkBuzz Hackathon is a hackathon series by BlinkBuzz in Kathmandu, Nepal. Every edition brings one real problem from one industry, and teams spend the day building answers to it. Each edition has its own audience, venue and date.

Edition 01

Edition 01 is for high school students from any stream, at TechSpire College, Kathmandu. Entry is free. The date will be announced here.

How it works

Register your team. On reveal day the industry partner opens one sealed brief. Teams build a working prototype, then demo to the partner’s judges for prizes.

Tracks

AI, FinTech, HealthTech, Robotics and Web3, plus new fields each edition.
