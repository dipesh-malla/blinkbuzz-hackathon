BlinkBuzz Hackathon: Nepal’s student hackathon series

BlinkBuzz Hackathon is a hackathon series by BlinkBuzz in Kathmandu, Nepal. Every edition brings one real problem from one industry, and students spend the day building answers to it. Edition 01 is at TechSpire College and is free for school and +2 students from Grade 9 to 12, in any stream.

How it works

Register your team of 2–4. On reveal day the industry partner opens one sealed brief. Teams build a working prototype, then demo to the partner’s judges for prizes.

Tracks

AI, FinTech, HealthTech, Robotics and Web3, plus new fields each edition.
